WindfoilDrawer

class WindfoilDrawer : BoundaryDrawer(source)

Draws shapes from a WindfoilMap with the windfoil algorithm. The coverage of a pixel is the box filtered coverage of the shape over the pixel, computed from the winding integral of its curves. Strokes are drawn from the distance to the curves within reach of the stroke.

minificationThreshold

open override var minificationThreshold: Double

Shapes that are at most this many pixels in width and height are drawn from the winding densities of their bands instead of from their curves, which is faster but approximate. Up to 1.5 times this size the two are blended. Shapes with a stroke are always drawn from their curves. 0.0 draws all shapes from their curves.
