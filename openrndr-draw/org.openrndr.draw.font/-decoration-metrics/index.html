DecorationMetrics

data class DecorationMetrics(val underlinePosition: Double, val underlineThickness: Double, val strikeoutPosition: Double, val strikeoutThickness: Double, val underlineFromFont: Boolean, val strikeoutFromFont: Boolean)(source)

The positions and thicknesses of the lines that decorate text: underlines and strikeouts (line-through), in pixels like Face.ascent. Positions are those of the centers of the lines, positive above the baseline, so underlines usually have a negative position. Overlines have no metrics in fonts, they are usually drawn at Face.ascent.
