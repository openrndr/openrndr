MathTable

interface MathTable(source)

The OpenType MATH table of a face, the constants and glyph information a math layout engine needs: fractions, scripts, radicals and stretchy operators. Lengths are in pixels at the size of the face, the units of the advances of shaping results, with y pointing up as in the font.
